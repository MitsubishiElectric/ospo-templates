# OSPO Templates

## Overview　

This repository provides documents that share our knowledge and experience related to OSS (Open Source Software) for use by both internal and external stakeholders.

We aim to organize useful information for various OSS-related activities including contributing to OSS projects, using OSS, and learning about OSS and to help create an environment in which people involved with OSS can work with greater confidence and effectiveness.

## Purpose

This collection aims to:

- Provide information for the appropriate and effective use of OSS
- Accumulate knowledge that can be used by a wide range of organizations and projects
- Continuously improve its contents by incorporating feedback from the community

We hope these documents will serve as a useful resource for organizations that use or manage OSS, as well as for individuals who participate in OSS activities.

## Available Documents

The following document is currently available.

### OSS Contribution Guidelines

These are guidelines for contributing to OSS projects.

They can be used to establish rules for submitting contributions to OSS projects, methods for proposing changes, and how submitted content is handled.

These guidelines are a template based on the knowledge and experience we have gained through our OSS activities. They have been organized so that they can be used by a wide range of organizations and projects. Please feel free to add to or modify them according to the size of your project, development structure, community culture, and other relevant circumstances.

- [OSS Contribution Guidelines](./oss-contribution-guideline/)

## Feedback and Contributions

This collection is not intended to be published and left unchanged. We aim to continuously improve it by incorporating feedback from the community.

We welcome the following types of feedback:

- Identifying missing or incorrect information
- Suggestions for making the content easier to understand
- Examples of how the documents are used by other organizations or projects
- Knowledge and practical experience related to OSS activities

If you have any comments or suggestions for improvement, please let us know through an Issue or Pull Request.

## Notes

The documents published in this repository organize topics that we believe are commonly relevant to organizations involved in OSS activities.

The rules and practices appropriate for each organization or project may differ. When using these documents, please review and adapt their contents according to your specific circumstances.

The documents may also be updated in the future. Please refer to the latest version when using them.
